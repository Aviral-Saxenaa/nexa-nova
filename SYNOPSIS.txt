Student Placement and Recruitment Management System

Project Synopsis

This project is a system for managing student placements and company
recruitment. It will help students maintain their profiles, view suitable job
openings, apply for jobs, and check application and interview status.

Placement administrators will manage student registrations, company details,
job requirements, student applications, interview schedules, and hiring
results. Companies can provide their information and job requirements.

The project is planned to be developed in Python.
